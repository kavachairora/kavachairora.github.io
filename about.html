About KAVACH

Our journey to make first-aid accessible to everyone

Our Journey

The Story Behind KAVACH

KAVACH was born from a simple observation: despite the critical importance of first-aid knowledge, many people lack the time, resources, or confidence to learn proper techniques. We set out to change that by creating a solution that makes first-aid accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or experience.

As a fledgling startup, we're driven by the mission of spreading first-aid literacy across all demographics. We believe that everyone should have the tools and knowledge to respond effectively in emergency situations.

2020

The Idea

KAVACH was conceptualized during a medical emergency when our founder realized how unprepared most people are.

2021

Research & Development

Our team spent a year researching and developing the perfect first-aid solution.

2022

MediPen Launch

We officially launched MediPen, our flagship product, to the public.

2023

Global Expansion

KAVACH began expanding internationally, bringing MediPen to those who need it most.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

At KAVACH, our mission is to democratize first-aid education and make it accessible to all. We believe in providing time-friendly, low-cost, and low-effort first-aid training to consumers of all ages.

We're committed to breaking down barriers to first-aid literacy through innovative products like MediPen, which simplifies the process and puts life-saving tools in everyone's hands.

"Every single person in this world has the capacity to save a life –It is an essential gesture of solidarity. First-Aid education should be accessible to all without discrimination"

-Grace Lo
