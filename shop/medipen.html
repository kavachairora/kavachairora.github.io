Simple Steps

How to Use

1

Twist to Open

Twist the top cap counterclockwise to open the MediPen. The cap will click when fully opened.

2

Clean the Wound

Use the antiseptic cartridge to clean the affected area. Press gently to release the solution.

3

Apply Gauze

Pull out the required amount of gauze and cut using the built-in cutter. Apply to the wound.

4

Secure with Tape

Use the adhesive tape to secure the gauze in place. The tape dispenser includes a built-in cutter.
